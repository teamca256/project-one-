Faculty of Health Sciences

Department of Public Health

The Department of Public Health prepares students to improve the health and well-being of individuals, families, and communities. Students learn how to prevent diseases, promote healthy lifestyles, and respond to public health challenges. The department combines classroom learning with practical training to help students understand real health issues and provide effective solutions.

Our experienced lecturers support students in developing knowledge, practical skills, communication, and leadership. We encourage teamwork, research, and community service to prepare graduates for successful careers in public health.

What Students Learn

Introduction to Public Health
Epidemiology
Health Education
Global Health

Department of Nursing

The Department of Nursing prepares students to provide high-quality care to patients in hospitals, clinics, and communities. Students learn how to assess health conditions, administer treatments, and support patients and their families. The department combines theoretical knowledge with hands-on practice to prepare graduates for the nursing profession.

Our experienced lecturers guide students throughout their studies and encourage teamwork, communication, compassion, and professional ethics. We prepare graduates who are ready to provide safe, high-quality nursing care and make a positive difference in people's lives.

What Students Learn

  • Fundamentals of Nursing
  • Medical and Surgical Nursing
  • Maternal and Child Health
  • Pharmacology
  • First Aid and Emergency Care
  • Business Ethics
  • Nutrition and Health
  • Nursing Ethics

Department of Pharmacy

The Department of Pharmacy prepares students to become skilled and responsible pharmacy professionals. Students learn about medicines, how they are made, how they work,and how to use them safely. The department combines classroom learning, laboratory practice, and practical training to help students gain the knowledge and skills needed in healthcare.

Our experienced lecturers support students in developing professional, communication, and problem-solving skills. We encourage teamwork, ethical practice, and lifelong learning to prepare graduates for successful careers in pharmacies, hospitals, and the healthcare industry.

What Students Learn

Pharmacology
Pharmaceutical Chemistry
Clinical Pharmacy
Drug Safety
Pharmacy Ethics
Human Anatomy and Physiology
