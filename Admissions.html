Admission Requirements

Make sure you meet these requirements before applying.

Education

Must have completed a Secondary School Certificate accredited by the Ministry of Higher Education.

Certificates

  1. Bring the original Secondary School Leaving Certificate.
  2. Include two (2) photocopies of the certificate.

Passport Photos

  1. Bring four (4) passport-size photos.
  2. Include two (2) photocopies.
  3. Photo background must be white.

Registration Fee

All programs: $25
Note: Registration fees are non-refundable.
