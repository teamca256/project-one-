Department of Business Administration

The Department of Business Administration prepares students to become successful business leaders, entrepreneurs, and managers. Students learn how to plan, organize, and manage organizations effectively while developing strong leadership, communication, and decision-making skills. The department combines theoretical knowledge with practical experience to prepare graduates for today's competitive business environment.

The department has experienced lecturers who support students throughout their studies. We encourage students to think creatively, work responsibly, and develop the confidence to face real business challenges. Our goal is to help every student become a skilled and responsible business professional.

Department of Finance

The Department of Finance prepares students to understand how money is managed in businesses, banks, and other organizations. Students learn how to plan budgets, manage investments, analyze financial information, and make smart financial decisions. The department combines classroom learning with practical skills to prepare students for careers in the financial industry.

Our experienced lecturers help students develop analytical thinking, problem-solving, and decision-making skills. Students also learn to use modern financial tools and understand the importance of honesty, responsibility, and professionalism in finance.
