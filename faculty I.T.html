The Department of Computer application helps students learn how computers work and how to create software, websites, and mobile applications. Students gain practical skills in programming, problem-solving, and modern technology.

Department of Cybersecurity

The Department of Cybersecurity prepares students to protect computer systems, networks, and digital information from cyber threats. Students learn how to identify security risks, prevent cyber attacks, and keep data safe using modern security technologies. The department combines theoretical knowledge with practical training to develop skilled cybersecurity professionals.

Students learn through a combination of classroom instruction, laboratory exercises, and practical projects that simulate real-world cybersecurity challenges. They develop the technical knowledge and problem-solving skills needed to identify security vulnerabilities, prevent cyber attacks, respond to incidents, and secure sensitive information across different digital environments.

What Students Learn

Ethical Hacking
Cyber Defense
Secure Software Development
Digital Forensics

Department of networking

The Department of Networking is committed to preparing students with the knowledge and practical skills needed to design, manage, and secure modern computer networks. As businesses and organizations increasingly rely on reliable communication systems, networking professionals play a vital role in connecting people, devices, and information across the world.

Our department provides students with a strong foundation in network design, configuration, administration, troubleshooting, and security. Students gain hands-on experience by working with routers, switches, wireless technologies, and network simulation tools while learning industry standards and best practices.

What Students Learn

Computer Networking Fundamentals
Routing and Switching
Internet Technologies
Network Monitoring
Cloud Networking
Network Troubleshooting
