Admission Requirements

Make sure you meet these requirements before applying.

Education

Must have Completed Secondary School Certificate accredited by the Ministry of Higher Education

certificate

1. Should bring the original Secondary School Leaving Certificate.
2. Include two (2) photocopies of the certificate

photos

1. Should bring four (4) passport-size photos.
2. Include two (2) photocopies of the photos

Background must be white.

registration fee

All programs = $25
Note: Fees are non-refundable.
