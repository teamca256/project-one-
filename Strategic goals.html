Strategic Goals

According to the previous five-year strategic plan, BFU has advanced immensely with its student capacity, international relations and social contributions and this next five-year strategic plan will build on that success and allow the university to reach its aspirations of becoming a research institution of international reputation.
BFU is now a well-established university with the following capacity parameters:

Excellence in Education

To enhance quality in teaching and learning through accreditation and program assessments. To improve teaching and learning resources To upgrade teaching Staff through the development programs To enhance the Student Experience.

Excellence in Research & Innovation

To boost research capacity in the University departments; To enhance the academic research culture and forge research and innovation partnerships.

Good Governance & Management

To improve the organizational structure and activities of the University to attain efficiency and effectiveness.

Relations & Partnerships

To establish strong collaborative relationships with National and International Institutions and organizations To reinforce the relations with international universities in the area of staff exchange,

Strategic Enrollment Growth

To increase the number of enrolled students who satisfy the conditions set by the university admission policy. To establish an effective public relations and Marketing strategy;

Infrastructure & Physical Facilities

To secure adequate space facilities for offices, teaching, learning, research, and other activities. To explore and attract local, national, and International (individual and corporate) funding for physical facilities.
